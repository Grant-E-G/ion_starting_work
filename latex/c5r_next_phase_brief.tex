{\LARGE\bfseries C5R: choose a control study before designing variants}\hfill 13 October 2026

\textbf{Recommend: E. coli MscL $\rightarrow$ osmotic-shock protection $\rightarrow$ paired shock/mock CFU survival.}
Conditional on existing stocks and routine plating. \textbf{No option currently passes both local assay readiness and predictive validity. All model-selected new variants remain NO-GO.} OmpF conductance is frozen; no further tuning.

Rankings assume no target-specific assay already runs at C5R. An established local workflow should take priority over rebuilding the nominally simpler one.

\textbf{Confirmed publicly:} CLARIOstar Plus, liquid handling and PCR/small-volume incubation equipment.
\textbf{Not confirmed:} target stocks, live-bacterial SOPs, CFU capacity, expression QC, or calibrated light/pH apparatus. The reader does not establish these workflows. No dollar estimate or purchase is proposed.

\textbf{Smallest pilot, only if materials/workflow already exist:} WT MscL rescue and empty vector in the same deletion host; mock/intermediate/strong shock; three independent cultures per construct on two assay days. Twelve cultures, 36 split-condition samples plus technical plating. Plan roughly 3--4 calendar days including overnight steps; C5R must confirm labor and scheduling.

\textbf{Proposed assay gate:} $\geq$10$\times$ WT/vector survival under strong shock on each day; countable CFUs with detection limits; viable mock controls without a consistent WT penalty. At intermediate shock, WT survival 0.2--0.8 and biological SD $\leq$0.10 absolute provide headroom/repeatability. Proposed thresholds, not results or a power calculation. Passing permits an assay-feasibility GO; \textbf{new designs still require a frozen predictor beating simple baselines on held-out known mutants, including useful gains and leakage penalties.}

\textbf{C5R response needed:} (1) Which matched strains/constructs and target SOPs already exist? (2) Is timed shock/CFU work routine, and who handles manual steps? (3) What expression QC and raw culture-level data can be returned? (4) Does an existing OmpF or calibrated GtACR2 workflow change the ranking?

If materials are absent, first obtain the existing-stock/SOP/control-data inventory; \textbf{do not order variants or start spending.}
Unproven: local execution, modest-gain sensitivity, mutant prediction and neuronal transfer.
