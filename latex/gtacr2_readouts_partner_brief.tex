{\LARGE\bfseries\color{ink} GtACR2: readout options for a small functional POC}\hfill October 12, 2026

\textbf{Aim:} prospectively engineer an improved functional membrane-protein phenotype using a repeatable workflow we supply to the partner. No patch-clamp dependency. A controlled transport proxy can support a bounded claim.

\textbf{Select by existing apparatus:} absorbance only $\rightarrow$ iodide endpoint; visible fluorescence $\rightarrow$ extracellular pH; red channels $\rightarrow$ voltage support; UV fluorescence $\rightarrow$ MQAE. Do not develop every route simultaneously or purchase a platform for the first batch.

\textbf{Bounded pilot:} calibrate the detector in representative matrix $\rightarrow$ WT / empty vector / known functional controls, dark vs light $\rightarrow$ independent preparations $\rightarrow$ four to eight prospective designs only after reproducible discrimination. Known pH-mutant ordering need not transfer to iodide or voltage. Avoid saturated endpoints and separate transport from abundance/damage.

\textbf{Claim:} improved assay-specific light-dependent transport or membrane-potential phenotype, with controls and reproducibility. These plate options do not establish intrinsic conductance, millisecond gating or better neurons. Reagent price is not full assay cost; quote required for most products. No assay has been run.
