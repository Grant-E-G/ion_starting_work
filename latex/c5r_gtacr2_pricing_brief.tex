\textbf{Publicly documented:} C5R's physical-task appendices name a CLARIOstar Plus reader, Hamilton STARlet and thermocycler; small-volume temperature control/heater-shaker equipment is also listed. No new plate reader is budgeted. Optional reader configuration and functional bacterial expression remain separate conditions.

\textbf{Budget basis:} control-only bacterial pilot, WT + known controls + empty vector. Shared preparation/controls \textbf{\$700--1,550 estimate}; readout-specific supplies added below. In-kind staff and existing reader/basic lab access. No new light, tax/shipping or open-ended commissioning included. Prices are whole packs, not prorated usage.

\textbf{Shared illumination:} if a compatible calibrated fixture is available, use it. Otherwise reserve \textbf{\$1,500--2,000 once}: LED listing \$261.20 + optical meter listing \$743.59 + estimated driver/power/fixture supplies. This is a bench setup, not a turnkey 96-well illuminator. Do not multiply this cost by every readout.

\textbf{Recommended bounded first batch:} HPTS + DiSC3(5), \textbf{\$80 headline detector purchases}, approximately \textbf{\$830--1,880} total with existing light or \textbf{\$2,330--3,880} with budgeted new light. Detector/matrix checks first; then reproduce channel/light-dependent functional controls before adding predicted variants.

\textbf{Interpretation:} budgets are planning allowances, not lab-service quotes or demonstrated assay performance. Reader possession is established; membrane expression and readout transfer are not. Quote-only entries remain incomplete. No partner follow-up is needed to identify already documented equipment.
